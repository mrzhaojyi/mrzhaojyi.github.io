3M • 2022 – Present

AI-Guided Process Control
for Nonwoven Manufacturing

Production lines that make meltblown nonwovens have historically relied on operator experience to start up and change over. I developed a real-time recommendation tool that combines dimensionless analysis with machine learning to guide operator settings and warn of problems early, and deployed it on a production line with live dashboards.

Found a lower-temperature operating window: ~2–3% energy savings, 10–20% longer die life, and hundreds of production hours freed each year.

3M • 2022 – Present

Digital Twins for
Roll-to-Roll Manufacturing

I build and maintain thermal and web-handling digital twins for several pilot and plant lines, connected to live sensor data and hosted on a cloud model platform. Engineers use them to run "what-if" virtual experiments instead of expensive pilot-line trials. The work covers thermal management, air-flow and pressure control, cooling-roll sizing and structural reliability.

Trained engineering teams to replace pilot runs costing $1.5k–$3k per hour with model-based validation.

3M • 2024 – 2026

Design-to-CNC Automation
for Multi-Head Waterjet Cutting

For a new abrasive belt product, I built a cloud-based tool that turns product designs directly into CNC-ready programs for a multi-head waterjet cutter, with automatic nesting to minimize material waste. I also used FEA to make the belt's joint design more robust, and supported the system through factory and site acceptance testing into production.

Raised line OEE from 65% to 75% while reducing manual labor.

3M • 2022 – Present

Engineering Analysis
for Capital Equipment

I'm the go-to structural and thermal analyst for equipment upgrades and new designs at plants in the US and Europe. Using FEA, CFD and heat transfer analysis, I check designs for deflection, stress and thermal limits before they're built, and lead safety-focused redesigns that remove known failure modes.

Helped protect multi-million-dollar product lines and remove serious safety risks.

3M • 2024 – Present

Process Scale-Up
with Physics + Data

Scaling a process from pilot to production is where small physics surprises become expensive. I combine thermal and process modeling with statistical analysis of production data to find the parameters that matter, and use particle-flow (DEM) and burner heat-transfer simulation to understand capacity limits in high-temperature equipment.

Guided a production scale-up in Europe and cleared capacity bottlenecks.

Schaeffler • 2018 – 2022

Prognostics & Health Management
for Bearings

As a Test Validation Engineer, I designed reliability test plans for global product certification and led root cause analysis and FMEA. I applied tribology-informed machine learning to detect bearing faults and predict remaining useful life from vibration and high-frequency acoustic emission signals (20 kHz–1 MHz).

Personal Project

Anomaly Detection
using Vibration Sensors

At the system level, a bearing fault produces a weak signal that's easily buried under noise from nearby equipment, so conventional frequency analysis struggles. This project uses an autoencoder trained on historical data to flag abnormal behavior and track damage over time.

Method

Hybrid Machine Learning
with Analytical Models

Pure black-box ML needs a lot of data and tends to overfit. Building fast analytical (physics) models into the learning process gives better accuracy and lower overfitting risk, which matters most when data is scarce or expensive to collect. This idea underpins most of my industrial work.

Method

Explainable AI: Feature Contribution
& Risk Analysis

Engineers need to trust a model before acting on it. Explainability tools such as SHAP show how much each feature contributes overall and how individual data points drive a prediction. That supports sanity checks of the model and data, and gives concrete design feedback that reduces risk.

Personal Project

Surface Crack Detection
with Deep Learning

Compares convolutional neural network models for automatically detecting surface cracks in images, a building block for automated visual inspection.

Ph.D. • University of Akron

Ultrasonic Peening:
Residual Stress Prediction

FEA of ultrasonic peening takes a long time to run, and the process has many interacting parameters. I developed an analytical model of residual stress, strain and roughness that agrees well with experiments, and paired it with data-driven prediction and optimization to recommend process settings.

Ph.D. • University of Akron

Fatigue Prediction of
Laser-Shock-Peened Parts

Laser shock peening adds compressive residual stress that slows crack growth. I built an FEA model that combines a high-strain-rate material model, laser–plasma interaction and mesh sensitivity analysis, and used stress intensity factors to evaluate the fatigue improvement.

  • Publication
Ph.D. • University of Akron

Electropulsing-Assisted
Material Processing

Electric current concentrates at grain boundaries, pores and phase interfaces, which causes selective heating. A cellular automata model that solves Maxwell's equations at the microstructure scale quantifies this effect, which can ease dislocation motion and heal pores in 3D-printed metals.

  • Publication
Ph.D. • University of Akron

Multiscale Simulation for
Additive Manufacturing

This work combines 3D printing with deep rolling to reduce porosity and refine grain structure. It's supported by multiscale simulation of macroscopic thermal behavior, plus microscopic solidification and recrystallization.

  • Publication
Ph.D. • University of Akron

Nitriding of
Ultrafine-Grained Iron

Combining ultrasonic surface treatment with nitriding gives much higher surface hardness than either process alone. I modeled the thermodynamics and kinetics to predict how the nitride layer grows, guiding a new low-temperature nitriding technique.

  • Publication
Ph.D. • University of Akron

Extra Toughness in
Gradient Nanocrystalline Metals

Gradient nanocrystalline materials stay ductile at high strength. I explained why from a dislocation-dynamics perspective and validated the explanation experimentally, giving guidance for designing lightweight structural materials.

  • Publication
Ph.D. • Timken-funded

Tribology & Rough-Surface
Contact Simulation

Predicted lubrication, friction, wear and fatigue of precision parts using measured surface topography. A fast, stable conjugate-gradient FFT contact solver handles rough-surface and asymmetric contact with random geometry.
